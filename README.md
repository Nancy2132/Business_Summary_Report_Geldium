# Business Summary Report: Predictive Insights for Collections Strategy

## Overview
This report presents predictive analytics findings aimed at helping Geldium's 
Collections team identify customers at risk of delinquency before their accounts 
become seriously overdue. It combines data-driven risk factor analysis with a 
practical, ethically-grounded recommendation framework for early intervention.

## Key Findings

**Top 3 Risk Factors for Delinquency:**
- Multiple missed payments
- High credit utilization
- High debt-to-income ratio

Payment history emerged as the strongest predictor of delinquency, followed by 
credit utilization and overall debt burden. Customers exhibiting unstable 
repayment patterns are the highest-priority segment for proactive outreach.

## Recommendation

**Proposed Solution:** An automated early-intervention program (SMS, email, and 
phone reminders) targeting high-risk customers, built on the SMART framework:

| Criteria | Detail |
|---|---|
| Specific | Contact customers identified as high risk |
| Measurable | Reduce 30-day delinquency by 10% within six months |
| Actionable | Use existing CRM and collections systems |
| Relevant | Supports Geldium's collections strategy and customer retention |
| Time-bound | Launch within one month; review after six months |

**Business Rationale:** Early communication is cost-effective and scalable, 
helping customers resolve payment issues before accounts become seriously 
delinquent — reducing financial losses while preserving the customer relationship.

## Model & Ethical Considerations

- **Model used:** Logistic Regression, chosen for its transparency and 
  interpretability, allowing staff to explain predictions using understandable 
  factors (missed payments, credit score, credit utilization).
- **Fairness:** Regular monitoring is recommended to ensure consistent 
  performance across customer groups. Historical data may carry bias related to 
  income or location, so fairness testing and balanced training data are advised.
- **Governance:** Human oversight, data confidentiality, and compliance with 
  responsible AI principles (transparency, accountability, fairness, privacy) 
  are built into the recommended process.
